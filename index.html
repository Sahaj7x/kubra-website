WHO WE ARE

Rooted in Faith, Built for Excellence

Jamia Khadeejathul Kubra Al Islamiyya was established over 20 years ago with a singular vision — to create a sanctuary of knowledge where the light of Islam guides every student.

Located in Maradu Nettoor near Vyttila, Kochi, the institution has grown into a complete educational complex providing free food, shelter, and education to hundreds of students including orphans from across Kerala.

Named after Sayyida Khadija (RA), the first believer and unwavering supporter of the Prophet ﷺ, we uphold her legacy of sacrifice, dignity, and commitment to truth.
